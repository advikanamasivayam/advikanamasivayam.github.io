For MASLAB 2025, I worked on the mechanical design and construction of our team's competition robot. I designed and built several of the robot's mechanical systems, including the frame, vertical lift, intake, and dumping mechanisms, which allowed the robot to pick up, sort, and score game pieces. Our team was awarded the **Wilkens Family Award for Most Creative Design**.

I also collaborated with the software team to integrate our mechanical systems with ROS-based controls, enabling the robot to autonomously detect and manipulate game pieces using YOLO-based vision.
