**Plated Heat Exchanger Analysis**
I used Ansys Discovery to run CFD simulations to analyze thermal and fluid properties of oil-air heat exchanger, while testing various gyroid geometries to analyze output differences:
- Pressure drop
- Temperature drop

Some geometry considerations
- Floating bodies
- Hole through the length of gyroid for copper anode for plating process
- FDM vs SLA print
- Fluid stabilization in entry and exit for simulation purposes

**SLA Printing DOE**
I experimented with various print settings to identify optimal parameters for dimensional accuracy using ELEGOO Jupiter 2 SLA printer to improve dimensional accuracy and print quality. Some features I experimented with were the following:
- Support structure 
- Exposure settings  
- Cure time
- Positioning

**Rotary Design**
I supported EOS metal additive manufacturing workflows from DfAM through build preparation and dimensional validation in rotary compressor applications. I used Creo to redesign components to account for tolerance requirements and adapted them to be printed using our EOS metal 3D printer. One such component was an adapter for a 20,000 RPM 12:1 gearing motor.
